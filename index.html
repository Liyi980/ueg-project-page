UEG: Unified Sampling-time Guidance for Controllable Echocardiography Video Generation with Heterogeneous Medical Condition

📄 Paper (arXiv) 💻 Github

Abstract

Controllable echocardiography video generation must accommodate heterogeneous conditions describing cardiac structure, motion, and function. We present Unified Echo Guidance (UEG), a sampling-time framework that reuses a single frozen unconditional video diffusion prior to control myocardial masks, trajectories, and ejection fraction (EF), without paired condition–video training or task-specific generator fine-tuning. UEG formulates task-specific consistency objectives using frozen differentiable measurement networks applied to the predicted clean video, and steers sampling through their gradients. For trajectory control, UEG introduces Anatomy–Motion Decomposed Guidance (AMD). Experiments demonstrate improved condition adherence across all three tasks.
